Africa needs more skilled software engineers, in mobile development, data science and beyond. **Factory24** is
Afrolynk's programme to close that gap: bootcamps and training, mentoring sessions and workshops, and a place where
young entrepreneurs meet the people who will hire, fund and work with them. It runs in **8 African countries**,
including Tunisia, Ghana, Kenya, Zimbabwe, Nigeria and Uganda.
